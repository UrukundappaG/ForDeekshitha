Optimization of Airflow System: Centrifugal vs. Axial Fans One of AMBIATOR's key recommendations was the use of centrifugal fans instead of axial fans, particularly in low-pressure environments where filters are present. They highlighted the following advantages of centrifugal fans for TerraTrunk's design: Higher Static Pressure Handling: Unlike axial fans, which struggle to maintain consistent airflow when encountering resistance (such as filters), centrifugal fans generate higher static pressure, ensuring efficient airflow. Improved Filtration Performance: Centrifugal fans are better suited for systems requiring air filtration, as they prevent pressure drops and maintain uniform cooling efficiency. Lower Noise & Better Energy Efficiency: AMBIATOR helped us select a backward-curved centrifugal fan, which operates at lower noise levels while maximizing energy efficiency, aligning with TerraTrunk's goal of minimizing electricity consumption. Based on this guidance, we selected a mid-range centrifugal fan model that balances airflow efficiency, noise control, and energy consumption, ensuring optimal performance.
